School Attendance Mentoring & Support

Better attendance starts with better relationships.

Attend Mentors provides one-to-one school attendance mentoring and family support, helping young people overcome barriers to education, rebuild confidence and improve attendance. We work alongside schools, students and families to understand the reasons behind absence, build trust and create practical routes back to consistent attendance.

Our Approach

Attendance is a symptom. We look for the story behind it.

Every absence has a context. Our mentors create space for young people to be heard, identify barriers and work on realistic next steps.

1

Listen first

We build trust and understand the young person's experience before deciding what support is needed.

2

Build the plan

Mentors help students turn challenges into manageable actions, routines and achievable attendance goals.

3

Stay connected

We work with schools and families to maintain communication, celebrate progress and respond when things change.

“We don't simply ask, ‘Why aren't you coming to school?’ We ask, ‘What would make coming to school feel possible again?’”
